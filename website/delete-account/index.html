Delete Your RawLifeFlow Account

RawLifeFlow: Juicing Daily

You have two options to delete your account and account-specific data.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app

  1. Open RawLifeFlow: Juicing Daily.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Confirm permanent deletion.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the website

Use the form below to submit a deletion request. We will process your request after verifying your identity.

Important: Deleting your RawLifeFlow account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play before deleting your account if you no longer want it to renew.
Manage Google Play Subscription →

What will be deleted

  • RawLifeFlow account (Supabase Auth user)
  • Saved juice history and ingredient logs
  • Glow Streak progress and achievements
  • App preferences and settings
  • Account-linked scan history and scan quota records
  • Device scan reservations
  • Support exceptions and reviewer grants

Data retained (with reason)

  • RevenueCat webhook event ledger — Retained for financial transaction record obligations. Retention period: 7 years (standard financial record retention).
  • External deletion job record — Retained until RevenueCat customer deletion is confirmed. Contains only the canonical UUID and provider name.
  • Device Recall bits — Managed by Google Play Integrity, not by RawLifeFlow. Account deletion does not reset device-level abuse-prevention state.
